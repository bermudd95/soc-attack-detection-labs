# Threat Intelligence Brief – Credential Harvesting Campaign

## Overview
This project focuses on the creation of an internal threat intelligence brief intended for SOC analysts and security leadership.

The objective is to demonstrate how external threat intelligence can be analyzed, synthesized, and communicated in a way that supports detection, response, and risk awareness efforts within an organization.

---

## Project Scope
This threat intelligence brief examines phishing-based credential harvesting activity commonly observed in enterprise environments. The analysis focuses on attacker tactics, potential impact, and defensive considerations rather than exploitation of a single vulnerability.

---

## Deliverables
- An operational threat intelligence brief written for internal security teams
- MITRE ATT&CK technique mapping to contextualize adversary behavior
- Analyst-focused defensive recommendations

---

## Key Focus Areas
- Phishing and social engineering techniques
- Credential harvesting and misuse
- Risk to enterprise authentication systems
- Detection and response considerations for SOC teams

---

## Intended Audience
- SOC Analysts
- Incident Responders
- Security Leadership
- Threat Intelligence Teams

---

## Files Included
- `threat-intel-brief.md` — Primary intelligence brief summarizing the threat, impact, and defensive guidance
- `mitre-mapping.md` — Mapping of observed behaviors to relevant MITRE ATT&CK techniques

---

## Skills Demonstrated
- Threat intelligence analysis
- Intelligence-to-action translation
- MITRE ATT&CK framework application
- Security communication and reporting

---

## Disclaimer
This threat intelligence brief is a simulated exercise created for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes. It does not represent live threat reporting.
